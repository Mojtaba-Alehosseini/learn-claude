## Dead or moved links
Nothing found while searching returned a confirmed dead page (404). Two things worth flagging instead:
- **support.anthropic.com** vs **support.claude.com** — the "Get started with Claude" Help Center article exists at both the legacy `support.anthropic.com` domain and the current `support.claude.com` domain with the same article ID but a slightly different URL slug. Both resolved when checked; treat `support.claude.com` as the canonical one going forward.
- The Medium version of "I Took 20+ Free Claude Courses" (by the same Artificial Corner author) is **member-paywalled**. The free Substack version at artificialcorner.com/p/free-claude carries similar content and is the one listed above.

## Looks outdated
- **"Introduction to Prompt Engineering for Generative AI"** (LinkedIn Learning) — released August 28, 2024, with the largest viewer count of anything in LinkedIn's Claude topic list (~389,000). It's a general GenAI prompting course, not Claude-specific, and at two years old it predates Cowork, the current model lineup, Skills, and MCP entirely. Found via: linkedin.com/learning/topics/claude — not included in the main list above because of its age.
- The **Real-World AI for Everyone** specialization (listed above) is only 9 months old, so it isn't flagged outright, but it launched *before* Cowork existed — worth knowing going in.

## Avoid
These showed up repeatedly in searches and have the hallmarks of SEO/content-mill spam rather than genuine instruction — generic marketing copy, no identifiable instructor, or explicit resale licensing:
- `thekitmarket.gumroad.com/l/jkats` — sold as a "21-part video course," but explicitly bundled with a **resale license** ("a license that lets you sell it, give it away, or use it in your own business"). That's PLR (private-label-rights) content, not original instruction.
- `moelueker.gumroad.com/l/ClaudePromptGuide` and `moelueker.gumroad.com/l/ClaudeAIWebSearchFree` — boilerplate "Mastery guide" copy with no identifiable author credentials, interchangeable with dozens of similar listings from other sellers.
- `sidstech.gumroad.com/l/pbyzhk` — a "free" lead-magnet PDF that funnels into a separate paid course upsell.
- `zipcadia.gumroad.com/l/pp001` and `theguywhodoesai.gumroad.com/l/ypexuk` — generic prompt/system-prompt products with no substantive preview and no track record shown.

## Gaps
What this audience clearly needs but nobody has quite made:
- **A verifiable, non-technical-friendly Reddit thread.** Despite several targeted searches of r/ClaudeAI for highly-upvoted posts by self-described non-coders, none turned up with a stable, checkable thread URL and real substance — only general subreddit references and unrelated news coverage. This may be a limitation of what's currently indexed rather than an absence of good threads on Reddit itself; someone with direct Reddit search access should double-check.
- **A single official "Chat vs. Projects vs. Cowork vs. Artifacts" comparison page for total beginners.** Anthropic's docs cover each piece well individually, but a newcomer has to piece the map together themselves. Independent creators (like the Ruben Hassid piece above) fill this gap unofficially.
- **Content for very low digital-literacy users.** Everything found assumes comfort with installing apps, navigating menus, and basic file/folder concepts. Nothing was built for someone uncomfortable with software in general (as opposed to just new to AI).
- **Accessibility-focused guidance** — nothing turned up on using Claude with screen readers or other assistive tech.
- **Calm, non-hype video content.** Beginner YouTube search results are dominated by "AI side hustle" and monetization-focused creators; genuinely low-key walkthroughs made for an office-admin or nurse audience rather than an entrepreneur audience are thin.
